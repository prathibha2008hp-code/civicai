## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-assurance public infrastructure, institutional stability, and modern computational governance. It serves city managers, municipal data scientists, agency heads, and active citizens who require absolute clarity in mission-critical decision spaces.

The visual ethos blends **Corporate Modernism** with **Analytical Precision**:
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Governed by structural alignments, meticulous data hierarchy, and an institutional posture without bureaucratic inertia.
- **Mission-Critical Utility:** Interfaces optimize for high scanability, low cognitive friction during high-stress operational incidents, and rigorous accessibility standards (WCAG AAA alignment across core operational paths).
- **Technological Foresight:** Tactful usage of computational surfaces, dense yet balanced telemetry modules, and geospatial map interfaces that position civic administration as advanced, intelligent, and proactive.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional credibility through deep foundational slates, accented by energetic emeralds that signify affirmative action and operational health.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Represents structural authority, command centers, primary typography, and persistent navigation anchors.
- **Secondary Emerald (`#059669`, `#10B981`):** Applied exclusively to positive civic telemetry, affirmative confirmations, primary action vectors, and live network metrics.
- **Operational Signals:**
  - **Warning Amber (`#D97706`):** Highlights pending infrastructural permits, network congestion, and non-blocking administrative alerts.
  - **Critical Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Reserved strictly for utility failures, public hazard dispatches, emergency alerts, and destructive actions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#64748B`):** Multi-layered neutral foundation that reduces eye strain in multi-hour dispatch shifts while preserving crisp contrast for boundary lines, borders, and contextual backdrops.

### Rules of Engagement
- Avoid gratuitous saturated color floodings; use color functionally to signal status, system states, and actionable levers.
- Data visual tokens (e.g., chart series, density chloropleths) must retain independent luminance steps to remain accessible under greyscale or deuteranopia conditions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headlines and commanding dashboard overviews with **Inter** for dense tabular records, administrative narratives, and telemetry labels.

### Structural Discipline
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with compact line heights and tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create clean, architectural anchor points across civic metrics.
- **Body & Data Dense Readouts:** Handled exclusively by Inter. Line heights remain generous (`1.4` to `1.5`) across multi-column data sheets to safeguard high scanning throughput.
- **Data & Telemetry Modifiers:** Tabular lining numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be active for all metrics, timestamps, coordinate displays, and civic budget figures.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-sm` utilizes subtle tracking expansion (`0.04em`) and uppercase transformation where critical operational labels demand immediate categorical distinction.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a responsive 12-column grid system calibrated for high-density analytical dashboards, situational split-screens, and GIS map engines.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with a `margin` of `2rem` (32px) and a `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px). Left-hand global navigation rails can remain fixed at `260px` or collapse into an icon ribbon at `64px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Secondary inspector sidebars convert into sheet drawers or slide-over overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout utilizing `margin-mobile` of `1rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Touch targets expand across interactive rows to satisfy minimum 44×44px hit bounds.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Interiors:** Metric cards, table cell paddings, and control groups use standard multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Standard analytical cards employ `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) internal padding.
- **Section Groupings:** Adjacent spatial zones (such as dynamic geospatial map canvasses and tabular municipal manifests) are separated by `space-xl` (`2rem`) gutters, reinforcing functional autonomy between modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines **crisp low-contrast outlines** with **ambient, slate-tinted shadows**. Rather than deep floating planes, the interface creates an anchored, structural sense of physical consoles and architectural strata.

### Layer Architecture
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`), entirely non-elevated. Serves as the substrate for page layouts and geographic spatial canvasses.
- **Surface Panels (Level 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and subtle ambient tint: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for persistent telemetry cards, data grids, and configuration modules.
- **Interactive Flyouts & Menus (Level 2):** Elevated white with crisp perimeter boundary: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Used for dropdown filters, citizen record popovers, and GIS layer controls.
- **Modals & Command Palettes (Level 3):** Floated overlays backed by an institutional backdrop veil (`#0F172A` at 60% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

### Geospatial Overlay Rules
Map controls, HUD counters, and boundary tools sitting atop dynamic GIS tiles must leverage translucent slate-white backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) enclosed with a crisp `#CBD5E1` outline to maintain readability over variable satellite and vector cartography.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This delivers a measured, structured visual identity appropriate for governmental documentation and municipal operations, avoiding excessive playfulness while eliminating harsh utilitarian edges.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Surfaces & Input Nodes:** Buttons, input shells, dropdown triggers, and status badges take a base radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-lg`):** Standard telemetry containers, analytical widgets, and map inspector dialogs take `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Major Modals & Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** Comprehensive modal dialogues, incident drawers, and floating command hubs take `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Exception for Status Chips:** Pure pill-shaped profiles (`9999px`) are reserved solely for live telemetry status chips and priority tags to provide immediate visual contrast against structural rectangular data tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Primary Navy (`#0F172A`) or Emerald Green (`#059669`) for positive operational triggers. Text in white, weight `600`, with a base height of `40px` (`space-md` horizontal padding) and `0.25rem` radius. Focus states exhibit a persistent 2px ring offset in `#10B981`.
- **Secondary:** White background with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#1E293B`. Hover transitions surface background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Deep alert crimson (`#DC2626`) fill with pure white typography, strictly deployed for emergency operations or record expungement.

### Status Badge Chips
- Compact, high-visibility capsules utilizing `9999px` border radius with paired light-tint backgrounds and saturated text:
  - **Nominal / Operational:** `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text and optional pulsing 6px indicator dot.
  - **In Progress / Warning:** `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text.
  - **Incident / Critical Alert:** `#FEF2F2` background with `#B91C1C` text.
  - **Administrative Neutral:** `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard base height of `40px`, `#FFFFFF` interior, enclosed by a 1px `#CBD5E1` outline.
- Active focus state introduces a border transition to `#0F172A` paired with a subtle halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`).
- Input labels leverage `label-md` in `#334155` positioned strictly above fields with `0.375rem` margin. Trailing units (e.g., "MWh", "m³", "USD") are styled in muted `#64748B`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Header bands are set in `#F8FAFC` using uppercase `label-sm` tracking with a bottom border of `#E2E8F0`.
- Data rows feature consistent 48px heights with clear cell borders. Alternating striping is avoided in favor of hover highlighting via `#F8FAFC`.
- Interactive cell selections highlight using an active border accent in `#059669`.

### Cards & Container Panels
- White canvas, `0.5rem` border radius, bordered with `#E2E8F0` and Level 1 elevation.
- Dividers between card headers, data viewports, and action footers employ hairline strokes (`1px solid #F1F5F9`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **GIS Map HUD Controls:** Stacked floating pill toolbars with frosted white glass, 36px square icon buttons, and coordinate displays rendered in monospaced Inter.
- **Municipal Telemetry Metric Tiles:** Specialized compact cards housing primary metric values (`display-mobile`), trending delta indicators (emerald/amber micro-badges), and inline sparklines.
